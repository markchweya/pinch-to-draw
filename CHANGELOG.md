# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-18)

- A see-through drawing overlay that covers every monitor, with Draw and Mouse modes.
- Pen, highlighter, line, arrow, rectangle, ellipse, text and eraser tools.
- Undo and redo, clear, and hide or show drawings.
- Whiteboard and blackboard backgrounds.
- Screenshots saved to `Pictures\PinchToDraw`.
- Global hotkeys.
- Pinch to draw with the webcam, using MediaPipe hand tracking.
- Smooth pinch strokes: a 30 fps camera, 1-Euro filtering, a 60 fps follower and tail trimming.
- The webcam picker skips dark infrared (Windows Hello) cameras.
