# Pinch drawing

## The gesture

- **Open hand:** a ring follows the point between your thumb and index finger.
  Nothing is drawn.
- **Pinch** (thumb and index fingertips together): the ring fills in and you draw.
- **Open again:** the stroke ends.

Every tool works with a pinch. With the line, arrow, rectangle or ellipse tool,
pinch where the shape starts and open where it ends. The eraser rubs out
whatever the ring passes over while you pinch. The text tool switches to the pen,
because typing needs a keyboard.

## Getting good lines

- **Light your hand.** A lamp in front of you works much better than a window
  behind you.
- **Stay inside the grey box** in the preview. That box covers your whole screen,
  so you never need to reach the edge of the camera's view.
- **Keep your palm facing the camera** and your hand about 40–80 cm away.
- **Pinch firmly.** The app waits for two frames of a closed pinch and three
  frames of open fingers. That stops a flicker from breaking your line.
- **Move at a steady pace.** Fast moves stay responsive, and slow moves are
  smoothed more.

## Mouse mode and pinch drawing together

Pinch drawing works in Mouse mode too. You can click through to your slides
with the touchpad and draw with your other hand at the same time.
